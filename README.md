FINAL EXPERIENCE

The visitor should never feel they are browsing a website. Instead, they should feel they have stepped into a living enchanted kingdom where every scroll reveals a new magical location, every click awakens another memory, and every animation deepens the emotional journey.

The experience should evoke wonder, nostalgia, mystery, romance, and timeless beauty. By the final scene, the visitor should feel as though they have completed an unforgettable fantasy film rather than visited a webpage. The story should conclude beneath an infinite sky of glowing stars, with drifting petals, fading orchestral music, and a sense that the journey continues forever beyond the screen.I actually think this project has evolved beyond being a "website." It should be treated as an interactive cinematic experience—something that feels closer to a Disney attraction or an Unreal Engine scene than a typical webpage.
